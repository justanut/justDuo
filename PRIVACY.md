# Duo Keys Privacy Notice

**Last updated: 28 October 2026**

Duo Keys is a keyboard for iPhone and iPad. This notice explains what it does, and does not do, with your data.

## The short version

- Duo Keys **does not collect, store on a server, share, or sell** anything you type or do.
- Duo Keys **never connects to the internet**. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no accounts.
- Duo Keys does **not ask for "Allow Full Access"**. iOS keeps keyboards without Full Access from sending what you type off your device.

## What the keyboard can see

Like every keyboard, Duo Keys handles the characters you type and the swipes you draw so that it can put text into the app you're using. This all happens on your device while you type. None of it leaves your device.

## What is saved on your device

Duo Keys saves a small amount of information in its own storage on your device only:

- **Your settings**, such as the layout you chose and the cursor speed.
- **Your own words ("My words")**. If you type a word that isn't in the built-in dictionary several times, or add one by hand, Duo Keys remembers it so that it can predict it later. You can see these words, edit them, add to them or delete them any time: hold the **123** key and tap **My words**.

Words are not learned from password fields, secure text fields, email or web address fields, number pads, or fields marked as a username, password or one-time code.

This information stays on your device. It isn't uploaded or synced anywhere, and it is removed when you delete the app.

## Information from Apple

If you download Duo Keys from the App Store or test it through TestFlight, Apple may collect usage and crash information under Apple's own privacy policy. We don't receive your personal information from Apple. If you choose to share analytics or send TestFlight feedback, that is handled by Apple and any feedback you write is seen by the developer.

## Children

Duo Keys doesn't collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
